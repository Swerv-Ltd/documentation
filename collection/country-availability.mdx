title: 'Country availability'
description: 'Country eligibility for USD collection account issuance.'
---

USD collection account eligibility is based on the account holder's primary residential or operating address. Approval remains subject to KYC or KYB review, document checks, sanctions screening, and product availability.

<Note>
The current Swerv public API flow for individual USD collection accounts is enabled for customers resident in Nigeria (`NG`). Contact Swerv support before building issuance for another country. The broader eligibility below describes the underlying USD ACH/FedWire program and does not by itself enable a country in your Swerv account.
</Note>

## USD ACH/FedWire availability

The underlying program supports individuals and businesses in most countries. It does **not** currently support USD account issuance for residents or businesses in the following countries and territories:

| Category | Countries and territories |
| --- | --- |
| Prohibited | Afghanistan (`AF`), Belarus (`BY`), Democratic Republic of the Congo (`CD`), Cuba (`CU`), Iran (`IR`), Iraq (`IQ`), Lebanon (`LB`), Libya (`LY`), Myanmar (`MM`), North Korea (`KP`), Russia (`RU`), Somalia (`SO`), South Sudan (`SS`), Sudan (`SD`), Syria (`SY`), Venezuela (`VE`), Yemen (`YE`), Gaza and the West Bank (`PS`), and the Ukrainian territories of Crimea, Sevastopol, Donetsk, Kherson, Luhansk, and Zaporizhzhia |
| USD ACH/FedWire unavailable | Algeria (`DZ`), Bangladesh (`BD`), Bhutan (`BT`), Burundi (`BI`), China (`CN`), Guinea-Bissau (`GW`), Haiti (`HT`), Japan (`JP`), Kenya (`KE`), Kosovo (`XK`), Morocco (`MA`), Mozambique (`MZ`), Nepal (`NP`), Niger (`NE`), Pakistan (`PK`), Qatar (`QA`), Tunisia (`TN`), and Zimbabwe (`ZW`) |

All other countries in the current provider matrix are eligible for USD ACH/FedWire account issuance, subject to compliance approval and Swerv product enablement.

## United States restrictions

- Customers whose primary residential or operating address is in New York are not supported.
- Texas is limited to first-party orchestration flows, and custodial services are not supported for Texas customers.
- Florida and Louisiana customers are supported, subject to the standard review.

<Warning>
Country availability can change as regulations and banking-partner coverage change. Confirm eligibility with Swerv before promising account issuance to a customer in a newly launched market.
</Warning>

For the provider's current source matrix, see the [supported countries list](https://apidocs.bridge.xyz/platform/customers/compliance/supported-countries-list).
